## What BattleTested means here

A manifest may *write* any tier it likes; the loader lowers `BattleTested` to `OfficialManifest` unless `protocols/battle_tested_evidence.json` shows all three of:

1. the program account exists on mainnet and is **executable**;
2. at least **1,000 successful transactions** carry its address, counted over a window the record states (the signature index answers for the address, not for invocation — axis 3 is what establishes use) — the same number `semantic_graph_store::thresholds::BATTLE_TESTED_TX` demands of evidence the node earns at runtime;
3. of at least **20 instructions actually observed** on chain, the manifest can name at least **90%** — a transaction count alone cannot show that the declared surface still matches the deployed one.

The measurements are read-only (`getAccountInfo`, `getSignaturesForAddress`, `getTransaction`) and reproducible with `scripts/battle_tested_census.py`.

## Where the instruction surfaces come from

Manifests onboarded in Round 18 were generated from each program's **own on-chain Anchor IDL** — the account at `create_with_seed(find_program_address([], program), "anchor:idl", program)`, owned by the program itself — so the instruction names, discriminators, account order, writability and signer-ness are the deployed program's own statement, not a third-party copy. Where the IDL states a PDA derivation it is carried across verbatim; where it does not, the account is left ungrounded rather than given a guessed seed formula, because a wrong formula flags legitimate transactions (the C26 failure mode). Generation is reproducible: `scripts/fetch_onchain_idls.py` then `scripts/onboard_from_inventory.py`.
